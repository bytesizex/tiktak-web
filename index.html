Official TikTok Content Posting API

Turn a topic into a published short — without an editing session.

TikTak drafts a script from a subject you supply, narrates it, renders a vertical video with your overlays and publishes it to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels as your own account. You keep the accounts, the API keys and every file it produces.

What it does

Script

Takes a subject — a news story, a keyword, an idea — and writes a short narration with a hook and a payoff, tuned to a target length.

Voice

Renders the narration to speech with a local text-to-speech engine, so the audio track exists on your machine before anything is uploaded.

Video

Composes a 1080×1920 video: captions, topic badge, logo and a green-screen background, sized for TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

Publish

Sends the finished file to the platforms you connected, with the caption, privacy level and playlist you configured.

How it works

Connect your accounts

Sign in to TikTok, YouTube or Instagram once through each platform's official OAuth screen. TikTak never asks for a password — it receives a token that you can revoke at any time.

Pick a topic and a length

Type a topic or let TikTak pull from a news or trend source you enable. Choose the target duration and the voice.

Review the draft

The script, the voice track and the rendered video land in a local dashboard. Nothing is published until the run you started reaches its publishing step.

Publish or save to drafts

Post directly to your profile, or send the video to your TikTok inbox so you can add sounds and finishing touches in the TikTok app before it goes live.

Platforms

TikTok

Content Posting API — direct post and inbox (draft) upload, using the video.publish and video.upload scopes.

YouTube

YouTube Data API v3 — vertical videos under three minutes are uploaded as Shorts and added to the playlist you select.

Instagram

Instagram Graph API — Reels publishing for a connected professional account.

Your data stays yours

TikTak runs on your own computer. Generated videos, scripts, captions and the tokens for your connected accounts are stored in a local database on that machine — not on a TikTak server. There are no advertising trackers on this website and no analytics cookies. The details, including what is sent to TikTok when you publish, are in the Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Does TikTak post without my approval?

No. Every upload is the result of a run you start. Draft mode sends the video to your TikTok inbox, where it stays until you finish it in the TikTok app.

Do I need my own API keys?

Yes. You authorise TikTak against your own accounts and, for script generation and speech, you point it at your own model or TTS provider. Your keys are stored locally.

Can I revoke access?

Yes — disconnect the account in TikTak, or remove the app under Settings → Security and permissions in TikTok. Uploads stop as soon as the token is gone.

What content can I publish?

Only content you have the rights to publish, and only content that follows the TikTok Community Guidelines and the other platforms' rules. Automated spam, fake engagement and misleading content are not permitted.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual runs in the dashboard, or remove the application's data folder to erase everything, including stored tokens.
